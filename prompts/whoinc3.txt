TASK: Implement an isolated WHO + AMG combined medical-evidence experiment.

PROJECT:
healthcare-agentic-ai

OBJECTIVE:
Add an opt-in evidence composition layer that retrieves medical knowledge from BOTH:

1. Existing AMG-RAG medical evidence
2. Existing local WHO Lookup collection
   - WHO Fact Sheets
   - WHO Questions & Answers

The Diagnostic Agent should receive both sources as clearly separated evidence sections.

IMPORTANT:
This is an EXPERIMENTAL ADDITION.
Do NOT replace AMG-RAG.
Do NOT delete or disable WHO Lookup.
Do NOT change the existing production/default retrieval behavior.
Do NOT modify diagnostic reasoning logic, grounding logic, critic logic, safety logic, abstention rules, or human-review rules.

==================================================
HARD CONSTRAINTS
==================================================

1. DO NOT use DDXPlus diagnosis labels at runtime.

2. DO NOT use benchmark/relevance labels.

3. DO NOT add hidden diagnosis information to queries.

4. DO NOT create synthetic medical facts or artificial evidence.

5. DO NOT modify the WHO crawler or downloaded WHO documents unless a compatibility bug is discovered that prevents the experiment from functioning. If a compatibility bug exists, report it before changing the crawler.

6. DO NOT modify AMG-RAG retrieval parameters:
   - embedding model
   - vector database
   - distance metric
   - top-k
   - retrieval gate
   - corpus
   - index
   - ranking
   - reranking
   - query compression
   - candidate generation

7. DO NOT introduce embeddings, vector search, BM25, reranking, or another RAG system for WHO.

8. WHO Lookup must remain deterministic/local.

9. Do not send the entire WHO collection to the LLM.

10. Only load WHO documents returned as relevant by the existing WHO lookup layer.

11. Preserve source provenance for BOTH evidence sources.

12. Do not merge WHO and AMG evidence into an unlabeled text blob.

13. Do not force a diagnosis simply because WHO or AMG returned evidence.

14. If evidence is weak, missing, conflicting, or insufficient, the existing uncertainty/abstention/human-review behavior must remain intact.

15. Do not change the final safety policy.

16. Do not make parallel cloud LLM calls.

17. Keep memory usage bounded. Do not load all 569 WHO documents into memory at once.

18. Do not modify unrelated production files.

19. Keep the existing AMG-only workflow available.

20. Keep the existing WHO-only lookup available.

==================================================
WHO LOOKUP BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Reuse the existing:

    rag/who_lookup.py

Do not build a second WHO search implementation.

The combined layer should pass a clinically appropriate, label-free query to WHO Lookup based only on available patient/case information.

The WHO query must NOT contain:
- hidden diagnosis labels
- benchmark labels
- expected answer
- DDXPlus diagnosis
- manually injected disease name

If WHO returns:

    NO_MATCH

that is valid behavior.

Do not force a WHO match.

If multiple WHO topics are returned, preserve them individually.

Each WHO document should retain at minimum:

    title
    url
    doc_type
    filename
    relevant text/content

If the existing lookup exposes more provenance, preserve it.

    provenance
    accepted/rejected status

Only accepted AMG evidence should be supplied to the Diagnostic Agent.

Do not convert rejected candidate audit records into accepted evidence.

==================================================
COMBINED EVIDENCE CONTRACT
==================================================

Create a small typed contract for the combined result.

Suggested location:

    rag/combined_medical_evidence.py

Use the project's existing typing/dataclass conventions if different.

The contract should conceptually contain:

    CombinedMedicalEvidence
        who_evidence
        amg_evidence
        query
        provenance
        status

Possible statuses:

    BOTH_AVAILABLE
    WHO_ONLY
    AMG_ONLY
    NONE_AVAILABLE

Do not invent evidence when one source is empty.

Example:

    WHO_ONLY:
        WHO contains useful evidence
        AMG returned no accepted evidence

    AMG_ONLY:
        AMG contains useful evidence
        WHO returned NO_MATCH

    BOTH_AVAILABLE:
        both contain accepted/useful evidence

    NONE_AVAILABLE:
        neither source provides usable evidence

The status is informational and MUST NOT bypass safety.

==================================================
DIAGNOSTIC AGENT INPUT
==================================================

Update only the composition/input layer needed to expose both evidence sources.

The Diagnostic Agent should receive clearly labeled sections.

Example structure:

    MEDICAL KNOWLEDGE — WHO
    --------------------------------
    Source: WHO Fact Sheet
    Topic: Asthma
    URL: ...
    Section: Key facts

    ...

    MEDICAL EVIDENCE — AMG
    --------------------------------
    Source ID: ...
    Title: ...
    Section: ...
    Passage: ...

The actual prompt format should follow the project's current Diagnostic Agent interface rather than blindly copying this example.

Important:

The model must be explicitly instructed:

- WHO and AMG are external medical knowledge/evidence.
- They are not patient facts.
- Patient facts come only from the patient/case context.
- Do not assume that a retrieved source proves the patient has a disease.
- Do not invent evidence.
- Cite only evidence actually present.
- State uncertainty when evidence is insufficient.
- Conflicting sources must be reported, not silently reconciled.
- Diagnosis remains a clinical hypothesis/reasoning output subject to existing grounding, critic, safety, and human review.

Do not make the prompt excessively large.
