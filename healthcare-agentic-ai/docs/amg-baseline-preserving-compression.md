# Baseline-preserving AMG compression — final evaluation

**Decision: DO NOT PROMOTE. Production remains exactly unchanged.**

Request: `prompts/new4.txt`. One fixed compression candidate, evaluated once on the saved `outputs/amg-live` case and the same consecutive `validate:2..6` cases used in new3. No post-result candidate tuning, additional retrieval strategy, model/gate/top-k change or automatic promotion.

## Summary: correctness is not retrieval improvement

- **Implementation correctness:** zero baseline-included clinical facts lost in all six cases. All 102 distinct baseline facts remain represented. Nineteen omitted facts are added, for 121/128 total clinical facts across the six records. Original answers, qualifiers, negation, unknowns and numeric scale values remain literal. This is deterministic representation coverage, not a claim that a neural embedding preserves every meaning equally well.
- **Saved-case improvement:** the mandatory 19-fact baseline compresses from 254 to 183 tokens. Adding all five omitted facts produces a 251-token query containing all 24 clinical facts. Anemia moves from rank 21 to rank 1; the other requested topics also improve in global rank/distance but remain outside both the top-five candidate set and the distance gate.
- **Regression:** validation case 5 retains every original fact, including chest/upper-abdominal pain and vomiting blood, yet accepted evidence falls from **five passages to zero** at the unchanged 1.10 gate. Other cases show mixed topic coverage and distance changes. This fails the requirement for consistent improvement.
- **Alias behavior:** no generated structural or demographic words enter the query; no `sex → Sexual Health` or header-triggered expansion appears in any candidate. The previous isolated ordinary-`all` guard is reused byte-for-byte, with no new alias rules. Existing source-backed expansions can newly fit after compression and are audited below.
- **Production status:** no runtime composition, backend, vendor source, PatientState, Patient Case RAG, Diagnostic Agent, Grounding, Critic, Safety, orchestration, clinical prompts, model, corpus, vectors, collection, labels, metric, top-k or gate was changed. The candidate has no production import or switch.

## Fixed implementation

`rag/amg/baseline_compression.py` exposes `compress_baseline(state, exact_production_query, tokenizer, max_tokens=256)`.

1. Take the exact query generated by unchanged `AMGMedicalEvidence.query_for` for each patient. Recover its fact membership by replaying the same complete-fact greedy budget rule over the three clinical collections, in production order. Validate exact equality with the supplied query; reject a stale/foreign/truncated baseline. Do not split patient text on semicolons or infer membership from substrings.
2. Reuse only new3's deterministic question-scaffolding compressor, unchanged. Preserve `slightly`, `recently`, `ever`, family relationships, timing, all alternatives/qualifiers and literal answer values. No medical dictionary, LLM, topic-specific code, diagnosis inference or synonym injection is used.
3. Group answers only when their **original question strings are identical**, keeping each answer. Unknown/unstructured questions are not collapsed into answered ones. Deduplicated facts keep all their original patient references.
4. Compress **all** baseline-included facts before considering any additions. If mandatory compression exceeds the token or character budget, fall back to the exact baseline with no additions. No fact is evicted to make another fit. No fallback was needed on these six cases.
5. Spend remaining capacity on previously omitted antecedents (including medication-taking facts) in original source order, followed by other omitted clinical facts in original source order. Skip non-fitting additions whole; do not truncate. This is a source-order policy, not clinical prioritization.
6. Emit only compact question/answer content separated by semicolons. No Presenting/Symptoms/History/Medications/Context headings, no synthetic `age =` or `sex =` fields. Roles and source references stay in the audit. Actual patient wording that happens to contain such words is not censored.

The complete PatientState is unchanged and remains available to downstream agents. The existing 256-token/4,000-character limits still apply. Tokens below include special tokens; actual embedded counts include any source-title expansion that fits.

The evaluation script is `scripts/evaluate_amg_baseline_compression.py`. Experiment code/dependency hashes were frozen before retrieval in `experiment-code-sha256.json`. There was **one candidate specification**, not a sweep selected by its results.

## Regression assessment and final decision

1. **Row 1:** clear information-coverage improvement with zero loss and Anemia now first. All requested-topic distances improve, but only Anemia is accepted among those topics.
2. **Row 2:** palpitations, chest pain, breathing difficulty, choking and the other baseline facts remain; six antecedents are added. Cardiac and anxiety-related passages now share the top five. Heart Attack and Arrhythmia remain, but both distances worsen and Chest Pain/Angina/Heart Disease in Women are displaced. No diagnosis correctness can be concluded from these unlabelled topic changes.
3. **Row 3:** all baseline facts plus five omitted facts remain; Mpox improves to first. Head and Neck Cancer and Lyme Disease enter, while Fibromyalgia and Temporomandibular Disorders disappear. This is a mixed topical shift, not proven increased relevance.
4. **Row 4:** three facts are added with zero loss. The same five passage IDs remain accepted (with ME/CFS/CML exchanging positions), but **every distance is worse**; Anemia goes 0.771755 → 0.848528.
5. **Row 5 — decisive regression:** the same 17 facts remain, including chest/upper-abdominal pain, nausea and vomiting blood/coffee-bean-like material. Applied alias expansion is unchanged (Nausea and Vomiting). All five new candidates exceed 1.10: the nearest, Heart Attack, is **1.103733**. Accepted passages fall **5 → 0**. No threshold adjustment, extra top-k or fallback triggered by retrieval performance was attempted. The unchanged no-evidence workflow would abstain if this candidate were used, but **no downstream run was performed** here.
6. **Row 6:** all eleven facts remain. Breathing Problems remains first but worsens 0.564340 → 0.598424; COPD, Lung Diseases, Asthma and Respiratory Failure complete the new set. Two previous asthma passages (including the child-specific passage) are displaced. Five hits still pass, but respiratory topical coverage/distance is not uniformly improved.

**DO NOT PROMOTE.** The zero-loss and no-generated-metadata requirements are implemented correctly, but preserving literal information does not preserve embedding geometry or accepted evidence. This single candidate fails consistent retrieval improvement, principally because of row 5's complete evidence loss. More facts, a better saved-case rank, and passing code tests are not sufficient. No clinical improvement, diagnosis accuracy or safety outcome is claimed.

Production remains unchanged regardless of this experimental verdict. No automatic promotion or new production flag was added. The isolated ordinary-all guard also remains experimental, as before.
